## Decisions requiring human ownership before relevant rollout

- [ ] Public QR policy/notice and optional-contact posture recorded by the owner.
- [ ] Immediate-safety route, out-of-hours backup and service levels recorded by the owner.
- [ ] Photo retention/privacy policy and the scanner or text-only public posture approved.
- [ ] Queue owner, QR printing/location ownership, pilot representatives, feedback owner and go/no-go date confirmed.
- [ ] Fresh Production readiness evidence gathered by an authorized operator before any mode/configuration change.
- [ ] Any authorization to open a public channel, deliver public notifications or widen rollout recorded explicitly.

The taxonomy is no longer an open decision for this delivery: the approved lean
package supplied the nine v287 categories. The administrator-role split remains
a separate governance decision; the same package defers it and it does not
block this implementation tracker.
